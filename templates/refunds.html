Refund Policy

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you're not satisfied with CodeDecipher, you may request a refund within 30 days of purchase.

Important: Refunds are only available if no tokens have been used. Once a token is spent, that analysis cannot be refunded. This ensures fair use of our AI services.

For reasonable circumstances (e.g., technical issues preventing use), we're happy to review on a case-by-case basis.

How do analyses work?

Each time you run an analysis (GitHub repo, code snippet, security scan, or API docs), it uses one analysis from your monthly quota.

What happens if I run out?

Free tier: You'll need to wait until next month or upgrade to Pro.
Pro tier: You can purchase additional analysis packs (coming soon).

Do unused analyses roll over?

No, your monthly quota resets at the beginning of each billing cycle.

How to Request a Refund

Email contact@codedecipher.app with your account email and reason for refund. We'll process within 3-5 business days.
